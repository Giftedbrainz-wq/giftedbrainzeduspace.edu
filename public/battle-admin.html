Battle scoring

Correct answers earn 10 points. Optional speed bonuses add 5 points for answers within 10 seconds, or 2 points within 20 seconds. The setting applies to newly created rooms only.

Battle Question Bank

Each subject has one question bank. Add or import questions under Mathematics, English, or any other subject. Matches randomly select approved questions from the selected subject.
